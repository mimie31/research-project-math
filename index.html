Tilapia Growth Analysis in Malaysia

In Malaysian aquaculture, monitoring the Red Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) is vital for food security. We model the weight gain using a simplified logistic-style growth curve over a 20-week production cycle.

Growth Function: \( f(x) = \frac{600}{1 + e^{-0.4(x-10)}} \)
Derivative (Rate of Change): \( f'(x) = \frac{240e^{-0.4(x-10)}}{(1 + e^{-0.4(x-10)})^2} \)

The Bioscience Problem

Farmers need to know not just how heavy the fish are, but how fast they are growing right now. The derivative \( f'(x) \) tells us the exact point where growth peaks (the inflection point). If the growth rate drops significantly, it's often a sign to harvest or check water quality/feed efficiency.
