Analyzing Cumulative Moisture Loss \( f(x) \) and Instantaneous Decay Velocity \( f'(x) \) in Tropical Cold-Chain Storage.

Food Security Scenario (Onion Supply Chain)

Malaysia is expanding domestic shallot production to reduce import reliance. The biggest challenge in agricultural logistics is cold-chain weight loss due to transpiration and respiration of onion cells. By tracking moisture loss following a sigmoidal curve \( f(x) \), we can identify the exact day decay velocity peaks, helping to prevent fungal infections like Aspergillus niger.
